The bet

One bet, repeated. Pick a preset, or describe your own. Win pays odds times your stake; lose costs your stake; whatever probability is left is a push (nothing happens, like a tie in Dice Duel).

    How to read this

    • Risk of ruin is the share of simulated paths whose bankroll ever touched the ruin level, even briefly. Paths keep playing afterwards so you can see where they end up.
    • The Kelly fraction is the share of your bankroll to stake to grow it fastest in the long run. For a 55% even-money bet it is 10%. It needs you to know the true odds: if you overestimate your edge you are effectively betting a multiple of Kelly.
    • Break-even fraction: staking more than about twice Kelly makes your bankroll shrink in the long run, even though every single bet is favourable. For the 55% bet the break-even is about 19.9%.
    • Median vs mean: a bankroll grows by multiples, so a few very lucky paths can drag the average far above what a typical player experiences. The median is the honest "typical" outcome.
    • Drawdown is the biggest fall from a previous peak. Even full Kelly means painful drawdowns, which is why many people stake half of it.
